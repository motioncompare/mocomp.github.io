# App Legal Pages — GitHub Pages

This is a simple GitHub Pages website for listing your apps and providing a Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and contact email.

## Before publishing

Replace these placeholders in `index.html`, `privacy.html`, and `terms.html`:

- `Your Apps` — your developer/company name
- `Video Compare` — your app name(s)
- `YOUR_EMAIL@example.com` — your contact email

You can add more apps by copying an app card in `index.html`.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Create a new GitHub repository.
2. Upload `index.html`, `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `styles.css`, and this README.
3. In GitHub, open **Settings → Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Select your main branch and the `/ (root)` folder.
6. Save.
7. GitHub will provide your public Pages address.

## Important

The Privacy Policy and Terms included here are a general starting template, not legal advice. Review them and update them to accurately describe what each app actually does, including any analytics, advertising, accounts, cloud storage, subscriptions, payments, or third-party services.

Motion Compare and Video Analysis App

MoComp is a video comparison and analysis tool built for anyone who needs to line up two clips and see precisely what changed: a pitcher checking arm slot between two deliveries, a golfer comparing this week's swing to last month's, a physical therapist tracking a patient's gait before and after treatment, a prosthetist comparing a patient's stride with different socket adjustments, or two product demo takes being reviewed side by side. If you've ever wished you could put two videos next to each other, scrub them together, and actually measure the difference — this is that tool.
